Tri-Party Feasibility Calibrator

Calibrate Product demand, Industry attractiveness, and Financial readiness. Achieve 70% overall viability across all three dimensions to solidify structural resilience.

Product Desirability 50%
Industry Attractiveness 50%
Financial Feasibility 50%
Deep-Dive Concept Explainer ▼
The Tri-Party Feasibility Framework: Validates venture feasibility in parallel tracks:
1. Product/Service Feasibility (P): Tests desirability and technical deliverability.
2. Industry/Market Feasibility (I): Investigates structural attractiveness (using Porter's Five Forces).
3. Organizational/Financial Feasibility (F): Tests human capital compatibility and foundational unit economics.

Let the composite viability score be calculated as: $$\text{Viability Score (ශක්‍යතා අගය)} = \frac{P + I + F}{3}$$ To secure feasibility verification, this index must satisfy $\ge 70\%$.
Step 3 of 4

The Agile Learning Cycle

Drag and lock the core artifacts of the Lean Feedback loop into the chronologically correct socket: Build (MVP), Measure (Data & Metrics), or Learn (Pivot or Persevere decision).

Deep-Dive Concept Explainer ▼
The Lean Startup Cycle (Eric Ries): A modern framework that minimizes market cycle times by organizing actions around Build-Measure-Learn loops:
- Build: Develop a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) with minimal resources.
- Measure: Gather customer behaviors and test structural hypotheses using actionable data metrics.
- Learn: Accumulate validated learning to decide whether to Pivot (fundamentally reorient) or Persevere.
Step 4 of 4

Unit Economics Balance Scale

Perform unit economic mapping. Set Total Fixed Costs (TFC), Price (P), and Unit Variable Cost (VCU). Then, calibrate Quantity (Q) until Total Revenue balances perfectly with Total Costs!

Fixed Costs (TFC) $2,000
Price Per Unit (P) $100
Unit Variable Cost (VCU) $50
Sales Quantity (Q) 0 Units
Deep-Dive Concept Explainer ▼
The Break-Even Point (BEP) Formula:
$$BEP_{\text{Units}} = \frac{TFC}{P - VCU}$$
Where $TFC$ represents Total Fixed Costs, $P$ represents Unit Price, and $VCU$ represents Variable Cost per unit. The denominator $(P - VCU)$ is the Unit Contribution Margin (දායකත්ව ආන්තිකය) which offsets Fixed Costs until the equilibrium profit of zero is achieved.
